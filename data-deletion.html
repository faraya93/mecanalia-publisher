Data deletion — Mecanalia Publisher

Mecanalia Publisher stores no data about visitors or third parties. It stores only authorisation tokens and publish logs for the owner's own accounts, on the owner's own computer.

To remove access: revoke "Mecanalia Publisher" in your platform's app settings (Facebook/Instagram Apps and Websites, Google Third-party access, TikTok Manage app permissions). To request deletion of any data linked to you, email contactomecanalia@gmail.com; requests are handled within 30 days.
