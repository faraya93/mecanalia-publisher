Privacy Policy — Mecanalia Publisher

Last updated: 1 November 2026. Operator: Mecanalia (@mecanalia). Contact: contactomecanalia@gmail.com.

What this app is

Mecanalia Publisher is a private, self-hosted tool run by the owner of the Mecanalia channels. It publishes the owner's own finished videos to the owner's own accounts (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook) through those platforms' official APIs. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we handle

  • Authorisation tokens for the owner's own accounts, issued by each platform when the owner signs in. They are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local database and local files, and are never shared or sold.
  • Basic account details returned by the platforms (account name, profile picture, account ID), used only to show which account is connected.
  • The videos, titles, descriptions and hashtags the owner chooses to publish, and a log of what was published and when.

We do not collect personal data about viewers, followers or any other third party. We do not use data for advertising, profiling or analytics, and we do not sell, rent or share it. Data is sent only to the platform the owner is publishing to, as required to publish.

Retention and deletion

Tokens and logs stay on the owner's computer until the owner removes them. The owner can revoke access at any time in each platform's settings (for example Facebook/Instagram "Apps and Websites", Google "Third-party access", TikTok "Manage app permissions"), and can delete the stored data by removing the connected account in the tool. To request deletion of any data connected to you, email contactomecanalia@gmail.com; see also data deletion.

Security

Credentials are kept in access-restricted local files and a local database, outside any shared repository. The tool is reachable over HTTPS only for sign-in redirects and media retrieval by the platforms.

Changes

Updates are posted on this page with a new date.
